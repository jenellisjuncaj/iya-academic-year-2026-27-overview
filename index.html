Counting Rules & Data Notes

Source differences, exclusions, and verification.

  • Common section rule. Count each semester + section number once, using published counted ACAD, IDSN, and PRIN rows with numeric enrollment and positive capacity. Exclude source-marked contextual rows, cancellations, invalid counts, and sections without capacity. Courses scheduled by other schools remain outside scope. Section counts are semester offerings, not distinct annual courses.
  • Cross-listings. Fall includes two contextual IDSN copies of PRIN 550 sections 34782 and 34785, marked excluded; count their PRIN rows once. Spring already deduplicates by scheduled section. Course titles may differ between semesters; differing titles are shown together. Do not merge distinct letter suffixes.
  • Existing difference. Fall’s metric code accepts finite zero-capacity rows; spring excludes them and explicitly handles USC cancellation flags. This overview applies the positive-capacity and cancellation rule to both. Any resulting difference from a source dashboard is flagged below; it is never silently adjusted.
  • Student credit hours. Preserve each source’s section-credit map: enrollment × the reported fixed units. Fall’s manually maintained map is not refreshed by its enrollment script; spring regenerates its fixed-unit map from USC. Variable or unknown units are omitted from SCH, not from Course Enrollments. A known zero-unit course contributes zero SCH. The maps do not distinguish omitted variable units from missing units, so no estimates are made.
  • Snapshot totals. These are two current semester snapshots, not cumulative registrations over the year. Course Enrollments are not unduplicated headcounts. Available Seats sums max(capacity − enrollment, 0) per section. Utilization uses total seat capacity, so an overfilled section may exceed 100%.
  • Refresh and availability. The semester repositories continue their existing USC API refresh around 6 a.m. Pacific. This overview imports their committed snapshots hourly, at 25 minutes past the hour, and during Netlify builds. Source dates older than 48 hours are marked outdated; import time never replaces source refresh time. If a refresh fails, a clearly marked last successful snapshot is retained. Without both datasets, annual totals are unavailable.
